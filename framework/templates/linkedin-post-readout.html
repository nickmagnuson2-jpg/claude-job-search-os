How LinkedIn defines these

LinkedIn's ads help page defines job seniority as "the rank or level of influence of a member account's current role in their organization," and says "Member account Job Titles are mapped to Seniority using LinkedIn's proprietary taxonomy" with "no AI modeling or inference." LinkedIn does not publish which titles fall into each level, so Partner, Owner and the other levels are LinkedIn's labels for each member's current title.

Company size is LinkedIn's "estimated size of the organization" (same page).

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a424655
