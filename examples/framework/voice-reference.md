> **How to build this file:** Label ~30-50 of your own real sent emails (a Gmail label works well), read them for recurring patterns, and distill the patterns into the Rules section below. Then paste 2-3 of your best real messages into each Exemplar category. The drafting skills generate by *adapting a matched exemplar* to the new recipient, not by filling a template with fresh prose — so the closer these are to your real voice, the better every draft sounds.

## Voice Rules

Replace these placeholders with patterns you actually observe in your own corpus. Examples of the *kind* of rule that belongs here (these are illustrative, not prescriptive):

- Punctuation signature: e.g. "no em dashes; inline hyphen with spaces ( - ); no semicolons."
- Emphasis habits: e.g. "uses 'particularly' for emphasis rather than 'really'/'very'."
- Sentence shape: e.g. "compound clauses joined with 'and'/'but'; short closers."
- Openers/closers you actually use vs. never use.
- Words/phrases that are tells you want to avoid (filler adverbs, hedges).
