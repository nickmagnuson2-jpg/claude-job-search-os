## Description

Own the product roadmap for Northwind's enterprise workflow-automation platform, working at the seam between engineering, sales, and customers. Partner directly with the enterprise sales team as the technical product owner in competitive deals.

## Responsibilities

- Own roadmap and prioritization for the platform's enterprise tier (integrations, admin, security)
- Serve as the product partner on the top 10 enterprise opportunities each year: technical evaluations, security reviews, executive demos
- Run discovery with customers and prospects to translate stated asks into roadmap bets
- Build the business case for each major investment and align engineering, design, and go-to-market
- Partner with sales leadership on competitive positioning and win/loss analysis

## Key Achievements

- Owned the roadmap for a product line that grew from $3M to $8M ARR over two years
- Acted as technical closer on 30+ enterprise deals; personally ran the product evaluation for the two largest logos signed in 2025
- Cut enterprise onboarding time 40% by shipping a self-serve integration framework
- Built the win/loss program that reoriented the roadmap and lifted competitive win rate from 38% to 52%
